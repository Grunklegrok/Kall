# Kall v0.6.0 — Career Growth & Education

Kall Growth helps a user describe the work they want and turn that direction into a staged, trackable plan.

## Included

- Career goals for entering, changing, or advancing within a field
- Target role, industry, level, location, time, budget, and context fields
- Existing Kall skills used as starting evidence
- Skill-gap and adjacent-role guidance
- Staged milestones across research, education, portfolio evidence, community, market validation, and job search
- Specialized game-art direction for concept, environment, character, technical, UI, and VFX paths
- Transparent user-opened web searches for career paths, skills, education, portfolios, communities, and current market requirements
- Resource saving foundation and progress entries
- Milestone completion tracking and plan completion percentage
- Dark Nordic Career Growth & Education workspace

## Product principles

- Guidance is directional, not a guarantee of employment.
- Web searches remain visible and user initiated; Kall does not silently treat search results as verified facts.
- Users can compare adjacent roles before committing to one path.
- Completed projects and learning evidence can later be promoted into Kall Identity, achievements, Resume Studio, and applications.
- Sensitive EEO, authorization, and private profile data are excluded from growth-plan generation.
